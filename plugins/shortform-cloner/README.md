# Selects Clips

Make a template from a channel's shorts, then turn any long video into editable
shorts that follow it.

## Make a template

1. Open **Selects Clips** and go to **Templates** → **New from reference
   shorts**.
2. Paste a channel link (YouTube channel or TikTok profile), one short, or a
   few shorts with the same look.
3. Press **Analyze**. Selects Clips takes the channel's most-viewed shorts,
   keeps the ones that share the look, and finds where the video, headlines,
   captions and logo sit. The Selects AI reads the colours and caption style
   and learns which moments do well on that channel.
4. Check the template and change anything: video box, background, text,
   pictures, captions, and the AI's rules for choosing moments and writing
   headlines.

Three built-in templates are included: News band, Full screen and Center box.

## Make shorts

1. With a project open, paste a video link or choose a video in the project.
2. Choose a template and how many shorts to make, or let the AI recommend.
3. Press **Make shorts**. Each short becomes a Draft where the video, both
   headlines and every caption stay editable. Export with Handoff → Export.

Transcription and the AI steps use Selects credits. When transcription starts,
Selects asks you to confirm the credits: approve it as soon as it appears, because
the request closes by itself after about 30 seconds. If it closes, press
**Resume** and approve it then.

Captions follow the speech: a caption ends where a sentence ends or another
speaker starts, and a long sentence is divided where the speaker pauses.

## Updating from 0.2

Templates replace the styles of earlier versions. Styles saved by 0.2 are not
used; make a template again from the same reference links.
