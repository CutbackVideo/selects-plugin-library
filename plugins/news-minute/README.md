# News in One Minute

Turns a news article into a one-minute vertical (1080x1920) news short in an editable Selects Draft.

1. Paste an article link (or the article text) and choose a style. The **Chosun Ilbo** style follows the paper's one-minute news shorts.
2. The app writes a headline and 8-14 narration sentences, then checks every sentence against the article (meaning, numbers, unsupported claims, wording). Each sentence shows the article passage it comes from.
3. Review and edit the script, then approve it. Nothing is narrated before approval.
4. The app shows the narration's character count and credits, then makes the narration with the voice you choose.
5. It plans a visual for each sentence (article photos with a slow zoom, number callouts, charts, stat and quote cards, text cards). The planner looks at the article images and says what each one shows before placing it.
6. Review the scene plan. The approved script and narration fold into one line each (open them to read or edit). The app builds a preview Draft from the plan ("Preview · <article title>", saved in the Project) and opens it in the viewer on the right. Each scene is one row with its time, a thumbnail and up to two lines of its sentence; picking a row shows that scene, finished, in the viewer, and Edit opens its type, photo and text. Describe what to change and rebuild, or edit scenes by hand; then press "Update the preview" and check the new frames. Until then the rows and the Draft's name mark it as an outdated preview.
7. Use this layout: only an up-to-date preview can be confirmed, and it becomes the edit Draft under its final name. It holds frame, headline, scenes, captions and narration as separate clips you can edit. The scene rows then go to each scene on the edit Draft; once the plan changes after that, the rows say they no longer move until a new preview is built.

Narration uses Selects credits. Editing an approved script needs a new narration.
