# News in One Minute

Turns a news article into a one-minute vertical (1080x1920) news short in an editable Selects Draft.

1. Paste an article link (or the article text) and choose a style. The **Chosun Ilbo** style follows the paper's one-minute news shorts.
2. Before writing, choose **ChatGPT subscription**, **Claude subscription** or **Selects credits**. A connected subscription is selected first (ChatGPT when both are connected); an explicit or last-used choice is remembered for this Project. Connect subscriptions in Settings > Connectors, then refresh the connections in the panel. The app writes a headline and 8-14 narration sentences, then checks every sentence against the article (meaning, numbers, unsupported claims, wording). Each sentence shows the article passage it comes from. Writing, checking, scene planning, feedback revisions, repairs and retries all use the same chosen provider. The selector stays available throughout the workflow. Subscription failures never switch to Selects credits automatically.
3. Review and edit the script, then approve it. Nothing is narrated before approval.
4. The app shows the narration's character count and credits, then makes the narration with the voice you choose.
5. It plans a visual for each sentence (article photos with a slow zoom, number callouts, charts, stat and quote cards, text cards). The planner looks at the article images and says what each one shows before placing it.
6. Review the scene plan. The approved script and narration fold into one line each (open them to read or edit). The app builds a preview Draft from the plan ("Preview · <article title>", saved in the Project) and opens it in the viewer on the right. Pick a scene and describe a change to its colors, illustration, text, layout or animation. The app reads the actual graphic, including manual edits, and changes only the requested scenes. A request beyond the template's controls can edit the scene's graphic code. Script, narration, captions, clip timing, transforms and other tracks stay in place. The edit is checked before one commit; a failed edit or a conflicting manual edit keeps the previous version. Manual scene fields are applied with "Update the preview".
7. Use this layout: only an up-to-date preview can be confirmed, and it becomes the edit Draft under its final name. It holds frame, headline, scenes, captions and narration as separate clips you can edit. The scene rows then go to each scene on the edit Draft; once the plan changes after that, the rows say they no longer move until a new preview is built.

Subscription limits apply to all BYOS LLM work; these calls do not use Selects credits. Without a connected subscription, Selects credits are selected. If connection status cannot be checked, refresh or choose an available source explicitly. When all AI providers are unavailable, sign in and use editing mode, then refresh. An older Selects app without provider selection asks for an update before running any LLM work.

Scene planning and feedback revisions use the same selected AI as script writing and source checking. ElevenLabs narration is separate and uses Selects credits. Editing an approved script needs a new narration. Article extraction, photo preparation, text-fit checks, timing, motion graphics assembly and previews run as code; the app does not generate new photos with AI.

New drafts place narration on **A1 (Main)** and the background on **V2**, with graphics above it. Existing drafts keep their track layout when a graphic is edited; narration already on A2 need not be moved.

An article chart can be redrawn and animated using labels, values, units and periods from the article or its attached image, even when those numbers were omitted from narration. Requests receive a separate source-fidelity check; unreadable or unconfirmed data leaves the current graphic in place. Review the resulting chart against the source before exporting. A generated program is validated by Selects, but AI review does not guarantee editorial accuracy or pixel-perfect output.

If the script, timing or overall layout changes enough to require a full build, the app creates a new candidate draft and retains the previous edit. Legacy previews without clip identifiers bind only when a graphic has an exact, unique name and time match; an ambiguous target is never guessed.
