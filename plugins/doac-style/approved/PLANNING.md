# DOAC Style editorial policy

Read the complete transcript before choosing emphasis. Favor a meaningful opening hook, vary typographic families across the argument, and reserve stronger motion for meaningful turns. Energy is an editorial guide, never a quota.

Cover all spoken words once and in order. Ordinary phrases use white Helvetica Neue Bold at 52px with 62px baseline spacing. Preserve coherent phrases rather than revealing every word separately.

Use only film-ready adapters. Preserve each template's casing, fonts, internal alignment, motion and number transitions. Keep whole emphasis blocks centered at (540, 1400) on a 1080 by 1920 canvas. Replace reference host yellow with white; preserve semantic red.

Do not use reaction effects without a reaction, or playful lettering in a serious argument. Validate ink overlap, bounds, ordering and complete coverage before saving any draft. Never silently substitute an unapproved layout when validation fails.
