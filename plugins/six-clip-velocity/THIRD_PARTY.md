# Third-party components

## Music

`assets/music.mp3` is an original instrumental (about 123 BPM) generated for this plugin with ElevenLabs Music through the Selects generated-media service. It was trimmed so a beat lands on every cut of the format and given a one-second fade at the end. It is intentionally different from the reference's track.

## Gallery preview

`preview.mp4` and `poster.webp` are a Selects export of this plugin. Their six videos were made for this preview: still photos generated with an AI image model, animated with Kling Video v3 Image to Video [Pro] through the Selects generated-media service. They do not depict a real, identifiable person.

## Tools

- React and Remotion APIs are provided by the Selects panel and effect hosts.
