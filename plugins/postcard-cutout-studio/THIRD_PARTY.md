# Third-party components

## Sounds

The sound effects in `sfx/` are derived from CC0 1.0 (public domain) recordings on
Freesound, trimmed, equalised, levelled and given a short stereo room:

- Panel shutters — "Pentax K1000 Camera Shutter.wav" by yfjesse,
  https://freesound.org/people/yfjesse/sounds/579883/
- Curtain ratchets — "ratchet socket wrench tool" by AlaskaRobotics,
  https://freesound.org/people/AlaskaRobotics/sounds/551497/
- Title ticks — "Typewriter snippet 02" by cabled_mess,
  https://freesound.org/people/cabled_mess/sounds/360602/
- Subtitle click — "35mm Film Camera Shutter With Motorised Winder (3)" by Fission9,
  https://freesound.org/people/Fission9/sounds/693869/

The tone and the riser are synthesised for this plugin.

## Music

`sfx/music-strings-v1.wav` was generated for this plugin with ElevenLabs Music
through the Selects generated-media service.

## Tools and services

- FFmpeg and FFprobe: https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html — used from the Selects
  installation or the user's `PATH`. No binary is included.
- Selects generated-media providers, including background removal, are external
  services with their own terms and billing.
- React and Remotion APIs are provided by the Selects Panel and motion-graphics
  runtime.
- The title uses the DIN Condensed and Avenir Next system fonts where available. No
  font is included.
