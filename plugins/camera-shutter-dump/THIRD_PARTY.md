# Third-party components

## Sounds

Each `sfx/shutter-v2-*.wav.b64` is one camera shutter sequence built for this plugin:

- Autofocus beeps: synthesised for this plugin.
- Mirror clack and shutter click: "Pentax K1000 Camera Shutter.wav" by yfjesse, CC0 1.0,
  https://freesound.org/people/yfjesse/sounds/579883/ (the six pitch/room takes prepared for
  Postcard Cutout Studio in this library).

## Tools

- Node.js runs `build-script.mjs` from the Selects panel shell. No binary is included.

## Gallery preview

`preview.mp4` and `poster.webp` are a Selects export of this plugin. Their twelve photos were
generated for this preview with an AI image model and do not depict real, identifiable people.
