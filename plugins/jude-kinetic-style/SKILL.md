# Jude Kinetic

Reference: https://www.tiktok.com/@judecalsado8/video/7532912841712471304

Open a talking-head Draft and choose **Apply Jude Kinetic**. If the Draft has no transcript yet, the first click starts Selects transcript analysis and continues when it is ready. The open Draft itself is styled and reframed to 9:16; no copy is made, and a rerun replaces the earlier pass. In Clip highlights a picked timeline is styled in place the same way; a picked video goes whole into a new Draft (`… · Jude Kinetic`), which is styled.

The plugin reads transcript word times, asks AI to group them into expressive lines, checks complete word coverage, measures face framing, then saves a 1080×1920 Draft. The cream card with the red circle appears exactly once, at the moment the AI judges strongest; instructions can steer where it goes but not how many there are. Lines of a block interlock as in the reference: each line sits right under the previous one and shifts sideways so tall letters rise beside a shorter line, a short white line may shrink to sit between two tall letters, and no two lines touch. Left-hand blocks are turned in 3D as in the reference (12° about the frame's vertical centre line, so lines slope down towards the speaker); each line's **3D turn** value is editable and 0 makes it flat. Every block rises about 80 px as it enters and eases to rest (**Entrance rise**, 0 turns it off). Where a left-hand line reaches a person, it passes behind them: Apple Vision person masks are made from the footage through each clip's framing and stored in that caption clip (**Behind the speaker** switches it off). A block stays in front when people would hide more than 25% of a line, when no person touches it, or when the framing cannot be read. A masked line is cut to its own region of the frame, so switch **Behind the speaker** off before moving it far; masks follow the footage as it was when the Draft was styled, so rerun the plugin after re-cutting or re-framing. Every phrase and card is a separate motion-graphic clip. Original audio stays unchanged. Drafts are filed under **Jude Kinetic**.

Select a generated clip to edit its text, font, size, colour, position, fade and word start times in Adjust. Word start times are seconds relative to that clip. Moving or trimming clips uses the normal timeline. Font size shrinks to fit the text-width setting when necessary. Positions are computed once when the Draft is styled, so after editing a line's text or size, check its neighbours and nudge X/Y if they now touch.

On failure, run it again from the original Draft. A rerun removes only clips labelled `Jude Kinetic ·`; other styles remain and can visually overlap. Undo uses the normal app history. Export through **Handoff → Export**.

Measured rules: [style.json](style.json). Poppins Bold closely matches the reference. Playfair Display Medium Italic is an approximation; the exact italic font is unconfirmed. Fonts are embedded, with OFL notices in `fonts/`. No B-roll, sound effects, grade, grain, shadow or zoom is added. Background music: Kevin MacLeod "Dark Hallway (distressed)" (CC BY 4.0, the editor's choice) from its first second at -5 dB, at most its own 53 s (2 s fade-out when the Draft ends first); fetched once to `~/.selects/plugin-data/jude-kinetic-style/music/`, imported into the "Jude Kinetic" folder, and replaced on a rerun. Credit in the video description: "Dark Hallway (distressed)" Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), Licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0 http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ Every shot is reframed to 9:16 whatever the Draft's canvas or earlier transforms were: footage is split at measured camera cuts and caption scene boundaries, then framed per shot on the face (centre crop when no face is found). Upper-side captions offset the face to the opposite side when the source crop permits. If other graphics exist, their timing is preserved without extra splits. Multiple-face shots use the largest face and produce a review warning. Continuous face tracking and original italic font identity are not claimed.

Requires macOS 14 or later and FFmpeg (Selects bundled copy supported). Node.js is downloaded on first use by `runtime.sh` (pinned, checksum-verified, shared under `~/.selects/plugin-data/_runtime`); faces and person masks use Apple Vision through `vision-helper.js` (JavaScript for Automation), so nothing is compiled.
