# Validation and current limits

The current panel places four original Project photos as eight independently editable **Image** clips in a new 720×1280, 30 fps, 266-frame Draft. The exported result is an MP4. The prior still-MP4-input implementation below is historical format evidence, not evidence about the new Image route.

The current public `overlayResource(Image)` path rejects photos. The panel invokes the editor's existing internal `TimelineMutation.run` / `Sequence.place` Image route, without changing client code. Native `addTransition` on Image endpoints caused `InvalidFrameError` in this Selects version; the panel instead overlaps the fullscreen Image clips for ten frames and applies the measured transition alpha/brightness in their visual Effects. The internal placement bridge is a version-sensitive compatibility dependency and fails closed if unavailable.

## Not yet accepted as an exact format copy

The original video has a measured transient brightness increase during fullscreen crossfades. The plugin now applies the common measured ten-frame alpha and gain curve through native transitions. The fitted original model still has roughly 1.3 RGB levels of mean residual per transition at the sampled resolution, and the grid-to-fullscreen exit plus complete mask geometry still need independent acceptance before calling the whole format matched. Source picture content and ornament silhouette are intentionally replaceable, so pixel equality across different photos is not the acceptance test. Audio/beat behavior remains deferred. Selects' native preview issue is separate from the verified saved Draft and exported file.

A fresh offline analysis of the native export measured the grid-to-fullscreen A exit at frames 130–144 (`format-acceptance/grid-exit-native-full-observation.json` outside this package). At 4.533333 s, the source's content-normalized gutter estimate is 0.787 and the diagnostic output's is 0.743; at 4.566667 s they are 0.853 and 0.887. The source-over fit has much higher residual on the original than on the diagnostic output, so these estimates do not identify physical opacity or justify another timing constant. The v4 geometry was exported and its bright top corners checked; remaining acceptance is the other mask edges plus the full transition and occlusion appearance across moving frames.

Keep the plugin labeled experimental until the remaining transition, mask-edge, and full-duration motion checks pass. Do not describe the successful B replacement as proof that the complete No.14 format is identical to the reference.
