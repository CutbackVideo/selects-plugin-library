# Third-party components

## Frame image

`assets/frame-v1.png.b64` (cream satin with a blank instant-film frame) was generated for this plugin with an AI image model. Its photo window was made transparent for this plugin.

## Music

`assets/music-v1.m4a.b64` is a 13.9 s section of "Lofi again" by omfgdude, CC0 1.0,
https://opengameart.org/content/lofi-again - time-stretched x1.179 (pitch kept) to 141.2 BPM and encoded as AAC for this plugin.

## Tools

- The panel decodes the bundled files itself (base64 and SHA-256 in the panel, written through the Selects FileSystem). No binary is included.

## Gallery preview

`preview.mp4` and `poster.webp` are a Selects Staging export of this plugin. Their seventeen photos were generated for this preview with an AI image model and do not depict real, identifiable people.
